Van Gogh’s Logs

Note what stands out. See what comes back.

Some things only show once you keep track of them for a while: how often something happens, when, and where. Van Gogh’s Logs is a log for exactly that. Quick enough that you actually do it, and smart enough to show you something back.

  • Capture it in seconds Tap ＋, type a line, done. Or log from a widget on your Home Screen. Anything that can wait, you fill in later.
  • Your own log types A bird you spotted, a run, an expense, an idea. You choose the fields: text, number, date, place, photo, recording, choice list, scale and more.
  • See what it adds up to Counts, trends over time, a calendar, a map, your latest photos. You put together what you want to see, per log type.
  • Repeats, but only when they hold up The app shows when something comes back at the same place and time, together with the chance that it is a coincidence. It only calls it a pattern when it really beats chance.
  • Fills itself in A field can fill itself with your location, the date of a photo, an event from your calendar, or your steps and sleep from Health. Only when you turn that on, field by field.
  • Locked when it matters Lock a log type with Face ID or Touch ID. It locks again by itself when you leave it alone.

It stays yours

There is no server and no account. Your log lives on your device and syncs through your own iCloud between iPhone, iPad and Mac. Nothing is collected, measured or shared, not even with me.
