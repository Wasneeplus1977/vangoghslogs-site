Privacy Policy

Van Gogh’s Logs collects nothing.

This is the whole policy. Van Gogh’s Logs is an app without a server: there is no account, no backend, and no data goes to the developer or to any advertising or analytics company.

Where your log lives

Everything you record (your logs, your log types, and photos and recordings you add) is stored in a database on your device. If iCloud is on, that database syncs through your private iCloud storage to your other devices on the same Apple Account. That storage is yours; I have no access to it and cannot look into it. Other people who use the app only see their own log.

The widgets on your Home Screen read a small copy the app prepares for them on your device. Backups the app makes also stay on your device.

What the app uses only when you turn it on

The app only asks for permission when you use a feature that needs it. You can decline any permission or revoke it later in Settings; the rest of the app keeps working.

Location

Only while you use the app, and only to fill a place field with where you are at that moment. The app does not track you and keeps no route.

Maps and addresses

For small maps, street and city names and searching for a place, the app uses Apple’s map service (Apple Maps). A coordinate or search term goes to Apple, as with any app that uses Apple Maps. Nothing goes to me. See the Apple Maps privacy policy.

Photos and camera

You choose which photos to add to a log; the app only reads those. If you take a photo in the app, it can also be saved to your Photos app. That is off until you turn it on for a log type.

Health

A field can fill itself with, for example, your steps, sleep or heart rate from Apple Health. The app only reads what you allow and writes nothing back. Health data is not used for advertising or marketing, not shared with third parties and not sold. It only lives in your own log.

Calendar

While logging you can point to an event. The app only reads the calendars you select, only uses the title, and never writes to your calendar.

Microphone and speech

You can record a voice note with a log. Transcription happens on the device itself; the recording does not leave your device, except as part of your own iCloud sync.

Face ID and Touch ID

To lock a log type. The app only gets a yes or no from the device; nothing about your face or fingerprint is stored.

Notifications

Only when you set a reminder yourself. Notifications are scheduled on your device, not through a server.

What the app does not do

  • No analytics, no crash reporting to me, no telemetry.
  • No ads, no tracking, no advertising identifier.
  • No third-party SDKs.
  • No artificial intelligence that sends your logs to a cloud service.
  • No account, no sign-in, no email address needed.

Getting in touch

The button on the support page takes you to the form on damonia.com, another site of mine where messages about all my apps arrive. What you enter there (your name, your email address and your message) is forwarded to my own inbox. For delivery I use Resend; that service processes your message to get it to me. Resend is an American company, so your message may be processed outside the EU. Nothing is stored in a database, there is no newsletter and no profile is built.

Van Gogh’s Logs is an app by Damonia. Responsible for this policy and the contact form: Benjamin van Gogh. You can reach me through the form on damonia.com.

This site uses no cookies, no trackers, no statistics and no ads.

Deleting your data

Delete the app and the data on that device is gone. To remove the synced copy as well, go to Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Storage and delete the data of Van Gogh’s Logs.

Children

The app is not aimed at children and collects no data from anyone, children included.

Changes

If anything in this policy changes, it will appear on this page, with a date.
