Frequently asked questions

Where is my data?

On your device and, if iCloud is on, in your own private iCloud storage. There is no server. See the privacy policy.

Does it sync between iPhone and Mac?

Yes, through iCloud, if your devices use the same Apple Account and iCloud is on. The app fetches new logs when you open it; the first time can take a while.

Can anyone else see my log?

No. Your log lives in your own iCloud, and every user of the app has their own, separate log. I cannot see it either.

How do I make a log type?

Create a new log type from the dashboard, give it a name and pick the fields you want to keep track of. Then start logging. You can always add or change fields later.

Why does the app ask for my location, Health or calendar?

Only because a field can fill itself from it, and only once you use such a field. If you decline, you fill in the field yourself.

How does locking a log type work?

A locked log type only shows its contents after Face ID or Touch ID. It locks again by itself when you leave the app alone for a while, or when your screen locks.

What is the difference between a repeat and a pattern?

A repeat is a fact: something came back at about the same place and time, shown with the chance that it was a coincidence. The app only calls something a pattern after at least three times, and when the chance of coincidence is very small.

I can’t find a log

Check whether a filter is on for the log type (at the top). If you use several devices, briefly open the app on the device where you entered it.
